# 🛍️ Customer Segmentation with RFM & K-Means

![pandas](https://img.shields.io/badge/pandas-analysis-150458?logo=pandas&logoColor=white)

Groups e-commerce customers into **actionable marketing segments** using **RFM analysis** (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) and **K-Means clustering**, then recommends a strategy for each segment.

## ❓ Business question

> *Which customers should we reward, which should we win back, and where should the marketing budget go?*

## 🔍 Approach

1. **Data:** about 15,000 transactions from 1,200 customers across 7 Pakistani cities (2024–2025). The data is generated by `generate_data.py`, so the project runs without downloads.
2. **Feature engineering:** RFM metrics per customer
   - **Recency:** days since the last purchase
   - **Frequency:** number of orders
   - **Monetary:** total spend (PKR)
3. **Pre-processing:** log transform (RFM data is heavily skewed) and standard scaling
4. **Choosing k:** elbow method plus silhouette score (`outputs/choose_k.png`)
5. **Clustering:** K-Means, with clusters automatically named by their RFM ranking
6. **Output:** segment profiles, a per-customer segment list and recommended actions

## 📈 Results

| Segment | Customers | Avg recency (days) | Avg orders | Avg spend (PKR) | Revenue share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🏆 Champions | 168 (14%) | 11 | 33.3 | 253,077 | **58%** |
| 💙 Loyal Customers | 472 (39%) | 38 | 14.1 | 49,305 | 32% |
| 💤 Lost / Hibernating | 560 (47%) | 250 | 4.6 | 12,959 | 10% |

**Key insights**

- **14% of customers generate 58% of revenue.** This is a classic Pareto pattern, so protecting the Champions is the top priority.
- Almost **half of the customer base is hibernating** and contributes only 10% of revenue. Win-back offers here should be cheap and targeted.
- Loyal Customers are the biggest upsell opportunity: they buy often but spend about 5× less than Champions.

**Recommended actions**

| Segment | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Champions | VIP perks, early access, referral rewards |
| Loyal Customers | Upsell premium categories, loyalty points |
| Lost / Hibernating | Low-cost reactivation campaigns, or exclude from paid ads |
